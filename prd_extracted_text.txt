AFYAMETRIX
Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Last Mile Frontline Surveillance Acceleration and Operational Intelligence Platform
Document purpose:This Product Requirements Document (PRD) defines the business context, operational problem, product scope, user workflows, functional requirements, and MVP delivery direction for Afyametrix. The document is intended to guide product strategy, stakeholder alignment, pilot implementation, technical development, and operational deployment of a frontline surveillance acceleration and operational intelligence platform for public health systems. It outlines how Afyametrix supports faster disease reporting, offline-first frontline workflows, district and regional operational visibility, and rapid escalation mechanisms that complement existing surveillance infrastructure such as SORMAS.
Product Information
Field
Details
Product name
Afyametrix
Product type
Web and mobile frontline surveillance acceleration and operational intelligence platform
Product stage
Minimum Viable Product (MVP) / pilot ready
Primary market
Ghana, with West and Central Africa as expansion markets
Core positioning
A Surveillance Outbreak Response Management and Analysis System (SORMAS)-native layer that accelerates frontline reporting and transforms surveillance data into operational decisions
Primary users
Community-based Health Planning and Services (CHPS) compound health workers, District Health Information Officers (DHIOs), District Disease Surveillance Officers (DSNOs), Regional Directors, Ministry analysts, and donor programme officers
Goals and Objectives
Reduce surveillance reporting delays from frontline health facilities to the district level.
Allow CHPS compound health workers to capture suspected or unusual disease cases offline.
Notify DSNOs and DHIOs without delay when an edge case or threshold event is reported.
Provide Short Message Service (SMS) fallback escalation when internet connectivity is unavailable.
Reduce manual reporting burden and dependency on paper-form reports
Improve district and regional outbreak visibility through dashboards, alerts, and automated reports.
Generate measurable evidence for government and donor reporting.
Assumptions and Constraints
Assumptions
Constraints
SORMAS remains actively used in pilot districts.
CHPS and facility-level workers need faster escalation workflows.
Internet connectivity is intermittent in some district contexts.
SMS remains a reliable fallback channel for lightweight alerts.
Ghana Health Service and donor stakeholders prefer complementary systems.
Connectivity and electricity availability may be inconsistent.
SMS costs and telecom integration may affect scale economics.
Patient privacy and health data governance must be validated.
Government procurement cycles may be slow.
Frontline training and change management may affect adoption.
Background and Strategic Fit
African public health systems have improved data collection, but operational decision-making still depends heavily on paper forms, phone calls, and manual reporting.
At the frontline, health workers in CHPS compounds may report edge cases through informal channels before data reaches official systems.
Afyametrix bridges the gap between frontline reporting, district notification, and operational intelligence.
The platform complements SORMAS, District Health Information Software 2 (DHIS2), and other public health systems instead of replacing them.
Strategically, Afyametrix turns existing digital health investments into faster outbreak awareness, better coordination, and visible operational value.

Core Features
Feature
Description
Offline-first frontline reporting application
Allows frontline health workers to capture suspected or unusual cases without internet connectivity.
SMS fallback escalation
Sends lightweight urgent alerts to DSNOs and DHIOs when internet access is unavailable.
District surveillance dashboard
Shows case counts, disease trends, reporting completeness, facility status, and alert history.
Threshold-based alerts
Triggers notifications for disease spikes, reporting delays, and predefined outbreak indicators.
Automated weekly reports
Generates PDF and Excel reports for district and regional reporting workflows.
Regional aggregation dashboard
Provides district comparison, hotspot visibility, and operational oversight.
Role-based access control
Manages access for CHPS users, DHIOs, DSNOs, regional leaders, ministry users, and donors.
Audit logs and data quality indicators
Tracks submissions, sync history, alert delivery, and data completeness.
Release Criteria
Offline reporting workflows validated successfully with pilot users.
SMS fallback escalation tested successfully with DSNO and DHIO recipients.
District dashboards load within agreed performance benchmarks.
Threshold alert rules are configured, tested, and validated with public health stakeholders.
Pilot users are trained and can complete core workflows without technical support.
Support process and issue escalation path are documented.
Success Metrics
At least 50% reduction in weekly DHIO reporting time during pilot.
At least 80% successful offline synchronization completion rate.
At least 90% successful alert delivery rate for configured urgent events.
At least 70% active dashboard usage among target district and regional users during pilot.
Measurable reduction in phone-call-only escalation workflows.
Improved outbreak detection or escalation response time where measurable.
Positive feedback from Regional Directors and donor stakeholders on operational visibility.
Exclusions
Advanced AI outbreak prediction models.
Full Electronic Medical Record replacement.
Complex machine learning forecasting.
Population digital twin modelling.
Full national interoperability orchestration at launch.
Large-scale multi-country rollout before pilot evidence is validated.
Strategic Note
Afyametrix should be positioned as a frontline surveillance acceleration and operational intelligence layer that complements SORMAS.
The strongest differentiation is operational responsiveness in low-connectivity African public health environments.
Future integrations should expand only after frontline reporting, SMS escalation, and district dashboards prove operational value.
Selective AI capabilities should be introduced only after baseline operational intelligence workflows stabilize.
